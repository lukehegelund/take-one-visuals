What it costs

Three packages, and I'd love to customize one if none of them quite fit your day.

  • Ceremony, $1,000. Up to 2 hours. Your full ceremony uncut, with clean audio from the vows.
  • Ceremony & Reception, $1,400. Up to 5 hours. A 2 to 5 minute highlight film, first dances and speeches uncut, and drone when the weather allows.
  • Full Day, $2,000. Up to 10 hours. Getting ready through the last dance, plus a 5 to 10 minute film.
  • Raw footage, add $200. All the raw footage from the day on a thumb drive.

No travel fee anywhere around Spokane. I'm based in Post Falls, about 35 minutes east, and I only charge travel past a two hour drive.

Questions Spokane couples ask

How far ahead should we book a Spokane wedding videographer?

Peak dates from June through September usually fill six to twelve months out. Off season and weekday dates are often open with a lot less notice.

Do you charge a travel fee for weddings in Spokane?

Nope! I'm based in Post Falls, about 35 minutes from downtown Spokane, and I only bill travel for venues more than a two hour drive one way.

How much does a wedding videographer cost in Spokane?

My packages are $1,000 for ceremony coverage up to 2 hours, $1,400 for ceremony and reception up to 5 hours, and $2,000 for full day coverage up to 10 hours. Raw footage on a thumb drive is another $200.

How quickly will we get our wedding film?

A short highlight is often ready within days, and several couples have watched one the same night at their reception. The full film comes in about two weeks.

Do you work with our photographer?

Yes! I plan around your photographer instead of competing for angles, and I'll reach out before the day if you can send me their contact.

Can we get the ceremony uncut, not just a highlight?

Yes. Every package includes your full ceremony uncut with clean vow audio. Speeches and first dances come uncut in Ceremony & Reception and Full Day.
