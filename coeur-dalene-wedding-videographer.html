A Coeur d'Alene wedding film worthy of the lake

Coeur d'Alene's lake, pines, and golden-hour light make it one of the most cinematic wedding backdrops in the Northwest. I'm Luke, the founder and filmmaker behind Take One Visuals, based right here in the Inland Northwest — filming CDA-area weddings as heirloom-quality cinema, not just coverage.

Why couples choose a local Inland Northwest videographer

  • No travel fee. Coeur d'Alene and North Idaho are inside my home service area.
  • Local timing know-how. Lake-area light and sunset timing shift fast in the summer — planning around it is part of the process.
  • Fast turnaround. Past couples have had a same-day preview clip so they don't wait months to relive the day.
